## The problem

What was wrong, who it affected, and how you knew. Two or three short paragraphs.

## My role

What you were responsible for. Be specific, and be honest about what was
teamwork and what was yours.

## What I did

The process, in the order it happened. Research, workshops, sketches,
prototypes, testing. Keep it concrete: what you did, and what it told you.

## Decisions

Two or three choices you made, and why. This is the part employers read
most closely. A decision with a reason behind it says more than a
screenshot does.

## Outcome

What came out of it. If it never shipped, say what the prototype showed
and what you learned. An honest ending is better than an inflated one.
